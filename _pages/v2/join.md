The lab of Prof. Ji Li and Prof. Zhili Deng recruits at every stage — faculty-scientists, postdocs, and technicians, as well as master's and doctoral students. We work at the interface of clinical dermatology and molecular biology, and we look for colleagues with integrity, a genuine commitment to research, a strong sense of responsibility, and a collaborative spirit.

          <dt>Who</dt>

    <h3>Eligibility and platform</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Faculty-track candidates</strong> who meet the criteria for the national “Four Young Talents” programs or Xiangya Hospital’s “Feifan Talent Leadership Plan” are encouraged to apply. For such candidates the platform, funding, and package are negotiable, with full institutional support for the relevant talent applications.</li>
      <li>All roles are anchored in national- and provincial-level key research platforms, with ample funding and well-equipped facilities.</li>
    </ul>
    <h3>How to apply</h3>
    <p>Send your application materials as email attachments to <a href="mailto:liji_xy@csu.edu.cn">liji_xy@csu.edu.cn</a> or <a href="mailto:dengzhili@csu.edu.cn">dengzhili@csu.edu.cn</a>.</p>
    <p>Please include:</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Personal information, degree certificates, and study / work history</li>
      <li>A list of research output (publications) with supporting proof</li>
      <li>Two recommendation letters from referees, one of which must be from your PhD advisor</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Use the email subject line <strong>“Position applied for + Your name”</strong>. This posting is open on a rolling basis and applications are accepted year-round; all personal information is kept strictly confidential.</p>
